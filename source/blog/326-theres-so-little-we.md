- I promise you do not need to watch the latest Spider-Man or play the latest Bethesda game
---

There's so little we in the global north can do to aid the historical struggle of Palestine. We can try to sabotage the military-industrial machinery, although that risks serious legal repercussions. We can send money, although many people have little to spare and finding trustworthy recipients is difficult. Or, for no money and no risk, we can choose not to buy entertainment products that [Palestinians have asked us to boycott](https://bdsmovement.net/economic-boycott). But for reasons I cannot fathom, even this is beyond so many people!
