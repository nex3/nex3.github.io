Here's a fun game to play: which director maximizes the ratio of "quality of feature film directorial debut" to "quality of the rest of their feature film oeuvre"?

I think Darren Aronofsky is the one to beat here. _Pi_ is quite good; it's got just enough science fiction to feel exciting and superreal, but it's otherwise quite restrained. His work as a whole[^1] is just the opposite: unsubtle and excessive, even masturbatory, lunging at deep questions but lacking anywhere near the kind of emotional or artistic maturity to actually address them.

[^1]: It's not all awful. _Black Swan_ is entirely fine. But then _mother!_ is a slap in the face to women and film as a medium, so it kind of balances out.

But Aronofsky can't be the only one who fell off a cliff after a great debut. Who else is in the running here?
